Why we exist

ProtPH is forbidden to design. Somebody has to.

PinoAI-ProtPH's release check proves it cannot generate, design or optimize — those capabilities are absent from its weights and code. This tier carries that half of the mission: it creates the new candidates that the analysis tier then screens crops-side. One program, two models, a loop.

Capability

Generative, by construction

It does

  • Generate novel molecular graphs (validity-checked — Molek-30M, our from-scratch MoFlow replica)
  • Generate novel protein sequences (ProtGPT2-small replica, ppl + pLDDT gated)
  • Design scaffolded binders / active-site backbones (served RFD4-Proteina)
  • Optimize molecules toward target properties, incl. crop-relevant descriptors
  • Inverse-fold and structure-guided redesign through the served design tool
  • Run the generate → screen → optimize loop as jobs (SciACA /v1)

How every claim stays earned

  • Numbers exist only when measured here — seed + config hash on each (G10)
  • Served models' benchmarks stay cited, ours stay ours — never blended
  • Design-capability language in public waits for the PPI benchmark pass
  • Structure claims ride the honest validator ladder (ESMFold → Boltz-2 → AF3-gold)
  • Release check mirrors ProtPH: generators in this tier, scoring heads in theirs

This tier has no "cannot" — only an honest ledger. ProtPH is the analysis boundary; we are the generative frontier it hands work to.

The handshake

Two models, one ecosystem

  • ProtPH grades the crops⇄GenD invents what grows better
  • ProtPH owns the crop corpus & benchmark⇄GenD owns generators & design tools
  • ProtPH: ESM-2 family, 14 PH crops, analysis⇄GenD: MoFlow / ProtGPT2 / RFD4, design
  • Governance 565d09 forbids ProtPH to design⇄This tier exists to design

The same 14 Philippine crops (rice benchmark + peanut, wheat, maize, potato, soybean, coffee, cassava, banana, cacao, tomato, mung bean, pineapple, coconut) meet this tier crop-first: rice is the first fine-tune arm here, exactly as it is the benchmark star there.
